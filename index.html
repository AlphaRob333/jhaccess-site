01 — Services

Everything between the ground and the work.

From a single frame tower to a fully engineered system scaffold wrapping a resort tower or show building, we supply the equipment, the labor and the engineering.

01

Scaffold rental & erection

System scaffold (ringlock and Layher-compatible), frame and tube-and-clamp. Erect, modify and dismantle by certified competent persons.

02

Shoring & re-shoring

Heavy-duty shoring, post shores and wall bracing for concrete, renovation and structural repair.

03

Suspended access

Swing stages, traction hoists, modular platforms and rigging for façade, glazing and exterior rehab.

04

Hoisting & material handling

Material and personnel hoists, loading platforms and debris chutes.

05

Overhead protection & containment

Sidewalk canopies, debris netting, weather enclosures and dance-floor platforms for occupied and guest-facing environments.

06

Engineering & design

CAD/BIM scaffold drawings, load calculations, tie plans and Florida PE-sealed designs. Permit-ready packages for CFTOD and local AHJs.

07

Stair towers & temporary access

Code-compliant stair towers, ramps and access bridges for construction sites and live facilities.

08

Event & entertainment structures

Stages, camera towers, truss support and show-set access for theme parks, studios and special events.

09

Safety training & inspection

Scaffold user and competent-person training, site-specific hazard analysis and third-party inspections.
